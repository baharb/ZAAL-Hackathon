Reporting a problem

To help us fix it quickly, please include:

  • Your device (for example, iPad Air or iPhone 15) and its iOS / iPadOS or Android version
  • The ZAAL version — it is shown on the app's opening screen
  • What you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead (a screenshot helps)

Frequently asked questions

Why does ZAAL ask me to sign in the first time?

The first time ZAAL opens, a parent verifies an email address or phone number with a one-time code, then sets a passcode (your birth year). The passcode keeps parent settings — like language and daily screen-time limits — out of your child's reach. Your child never needs to sign in.

I didn't receive my verification code.

Check your spam or junk folder, wait a minute, then tap Resend Code. Text messages cannot be delivered to Iranian (+98) phone numbers, so please use an email address instead. If the code still doesn't arrive, email us.

I forgot my parent passcode. How do I reset it?

On the passcode screen, tap Forgot your passcode?, verify your email or phone again with a new code, and set a new passcode. If you no longer have access to that email or phone, contact us.

There is no sound.

Turn up the device volume and make sure it isn't muted (on iPhone and some iPads, check the silent switch or Control Center). If you use Bluetooth headphones or a speaker, make sure they are connected and turned up.

Does ZAAL need an internet connection?

Only for the first-time parent sign-in and for resetting a passcode. Games, stories, and lessons work offline.

Is ZAAL free?

Yes. Every game, story, and activity is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad, and Android phones and tablets. ZAAL is designed to be held sideways (landscape) and looks best that way.

Where is my child's progress saved?

Stars, badges, and lesson progress are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes that progress.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email us from the address (or with the phone number) you signed in with and ask for deletion — see Requesting account deletion in our Privacy Policy. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.
