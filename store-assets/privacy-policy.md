# ZAAL Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 2026
**App:** ZAAL — Persian (Farsi) learning for kids
**Contact:** bahar.bahreman@gmail.com

This policy covers the current, free version of ZAAL. If subscriptions are turned back on later, this policy must be updated to also disclose purchase data collected by RevenueCat/App Store/Google Play before that version is submitted.

## What we collect

**Parent contact info (email or phone).** During account setup, a parent enters an email address or phone number. We send a one-time 6-digit verification code to it via Supabase (our backend provider) to confirm the parent owns it. This is the only server-side data ZAAL collects. We do not use it for marketing and do not share it with advertisers.

**App progress and settings.** Your child's age group, chosen language, stars, badges, stickers, and lesson progress are saved only on your device (local storage). This information is never sent to us or to any third party.

**Nothing else.** ZAAL has no advertising SDKs and no analytics/tracking SDKs of any kind. We do not collect location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data. We do not use your device's advertising identifier.

## How verification codes are handled

Verification codes are sent and checked through Supabase Auth. Once a code is confirmed, the app does not keep an ongoing session with Supabase — but Supabase does retain a record of the email/phone used to send that code, per Supabase's own data retention practices. We do not access, read, or use this record for anything beyond the original verification.

## Children's privacy

ZAAL is designed for children, and is used under a parent or guardian's supervision. Only an adult can complete account setup (the birth-year check requires an age of 13 or older). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children, and no child-directed screen in the app asks for contact information.

## Your choices

You can stop using ZAAL and delete it at any time; all locally-stored progress (your child's age group, language, stars, badges, stickers, and lesson progress) is removed automatically the moment the app is uninstalled, since none of it is ever sent to us.

### Requesting account deletion

To delete your ZAAL parent account and the contact record described above, email **bahar.bahreman@gmail.com** with the subject line "Delete my ZAAL account," sent from (or naming) the same email address or phone number you used to set up the account.

- **What we delete:** the verification record tied to your email/phone (held by Supabase, our backend provider, on our behalf) and any parent-settings passcode associated with it.
- **What's not affected:** locally-stored app progress on your device isn't something we hold, so there's nothing on our side to delete beyond the contact record above; simply deleting the app removes that data from your device.
- **Timeline:** we will process deletion requests within 30 days. Some minimal information may be retained briefly beyond that for fraud prevention or legal compliance before final removal.

## Changes to this policy

If ZAAL's data collection changes (for example, when subscriptions are reintroduced), this page will be updated and the "Effective date" above will change accordingly.
