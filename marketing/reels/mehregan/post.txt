Your kids know Halloween… but do they know Mehregan? 🍂
Mehregan is the Persian festival of kindness & friendship, on October 8 (16 Mehr).
This year, teach them one phrase: "Mehretan afzoon" (may your kindness grow) 💛

Save this for October 8 and share it with a Persian parent 🧡
