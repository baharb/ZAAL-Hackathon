Halloween in Farsi! Learn 10 spooky words with Neli, guess them, and say them out loud 🎤
Round 1: guess and learn each word · Round 2: your turn, "What is it?" · Round 3: all together!

In this video:
🎃 کدو تنبل (kadoo tanbal): pumpkin
👻 روح (rooh): ghost
🧙‍♀️ جادوگر (jâdoogar): witch
🦇 خفاش (khoffâsh): bat
🍬 آب‌نبات (âbnabât): candy
🕷️ عنکبوت (ankaboot): spider
🌙 ماه (mâh): moon
🧹 جارو (jâroo): broom
🐈‍⬛ گربه (gorbe): cat
🕯️ شمع (sham): candle

Tip for parents: in round 2, when the 🎤 appears, let your child say the word before it is revealed. Try the words again while trick-or-treating!
