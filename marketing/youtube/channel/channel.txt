DESCRIPTION (Studio > Customization > Basic info)
-------------------------------------------------
زال: فارسی، با بازی و قصه 💜
ویدیوهای شاد و کوتاه برای بچه‌هایی که خارج از ایران بزرگ می‌شن: با نلی و مامان‌بزرگ کلمه‌های فارسی رو یاد بگیرید، تکرار کنید و با خانواده فارسی حرف بزنید.

ZAAL helps kids growing up outside Iran learn Farsi through play.
Join Neli and Grandma for short, cheerful episodes: new words, real conversations, and a turn for your little one to answer in Farsi.

🆕 A new episode every week

SETTINGS
--------
- Studio > Settings > Channel > Advanced: "Yes, set this channel as made for kids" (every video on it is for children).
- Studio > Settings > Channel > Basic info: country, and keywords:
  learn farsi, farsi for kids, persian for kids, آموزش فارسی کودکان, فارسی برای کودکان, bilingual kids, iranian kids, ZAAL
- Studio > Settings > Channel > Feature eligibility: verify by phone (needed for custom thumbnails).
- Studio > Settings > Upload defaults: category Education, language Persian, so every upload starts with them.
- Customization > Layout: put the "Grandma's Call" playlist first.
